Model Answer

Speaking Example: Personal Opinion

Notice how connectors are used to build a coherent, high-level argument

From my perspective, students should study harder and complete their homework because education is one of the most important tools for their future. I strongly believe that regular study habits help learners become more responsible and independent. Furthermore, doing homework allows students to practise what they have learned in class and improve their weaknesses. Moreover, consistent effort can increase confidence and academic success. However, some people argue that too much homework can create stress and reduce free time. I beg to differ because I believe that a balanced amount of homework is necessary for students' development. Nevertheless, teachers should consider students' needs and avoid giving excessive tasks. Coupled with this, families and schools should encourage students to create good study routines. All things considered, students who work harder and take their responsibilities seriously have better opportunities to achieve their goals.
